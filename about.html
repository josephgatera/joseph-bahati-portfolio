MY STORY

A little about me

My name is Joseph Gatera Bahati. I was born on 2 February 2004 in Bambo, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. My life and family background have helped shape my appreciation for learning, perseverance, and hope.

I am pursuing my studies in networking administration. I am interested in learning how computers communicate, how networks are configured, and how technology can help people solve problems. Through my studies and practice, I want to develop the knowledge and discipline needed for a career in information technology.

This portfolio is a record of my learning journey. I plan to keep improving my skills, completing practical exercises, and learning from mistakes as I gain experience.

My goal is to learn step by step, work hard, and use my knowledge to create useful solutions.
